## The CROA Project

**CROA — Constrained Reachability Orchestration Architecture**, by **The CROA Project**.
The v1.0.1 citation names **Yasmine Durand, Darrin Smith and Sylvain Durand**.

- [Original repository](https://github.com/CROA-Project/CROA)
- [Requested v1.0.1](https://github.com/CROA-Project/CROA/tree/v1.0.1)
- [Pinned source](https://github.com/CROA-Project/CROA/tree/4d59192da9a0d00412c9d2ccb9e5ab68176803ff)
- [Website](https://www.croaframework.org/) and [DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22310276)
- [Æion CROA assessment kit](https://github.com/OualidGuennif/aeion-croa)

CROA specification/documentation uses CC BY 4.0; upstream code/machine-readable artifacts use
Apache-2.0 as recorded by the pinned Æion kit. SpaceShip proposes its own implementation mapping
and links to the framework. It does not bundle the specification, claim an official assessment,
relicense upstream work or assign itself a conformance level.

Original SpaceShip plan prose and inactive examples are MIT. Exact upstream hashes in the research
lock are provenance metadata, not a substitute for retaining licences when implementations are
later redistributed. Interpretation and integration mistakes in this plan belong to Æion.
